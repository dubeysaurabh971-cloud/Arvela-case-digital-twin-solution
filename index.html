Model assumptions

Weekly model over 52 weeks, in thousands of bottles. Demand is 52k a week, 72.8k in week 9 (promotion). Line 3 fills 52k a week, so it has no spare capacity. Finished goods start at 3 weeks of cover (156k) and the plant tries to refill to that level. The 150k finished old bottles at the supplier are available from week 1, the 80k in work from week 2, and the open order arrives in week 5.

Supplier output for new bottles: 25k a week from week 4, 45k from week 6, 60k from week 8. When Spain is active it takes 20k first. A pack reaches the shelf about 3 weeks after filling. The mixed-shelf period is from the first new pack on shelf to the last old pack, at most 6 weeks.

Costs: old bottle $0.21. Lost sales cost $1.11 of margin per unit. Each week below 2 weeks of cover adds a $5k risk charge. Holding new bottles costs $2 per thousand per week. Peak storage is shown in pallets at 1,500 bottles per pallet. A real twin would also run many scenarios for supplier ramp-up and trial success; this one is deterministic.
